STARFLEET

Your run, thoughtfully planned.

Starfleet is a personal Telegram assistant for planning running routes and managing a connected training calendar. It interprets route preferences, calculates pedestrian routes and elevation, and provides links to view routes on OnTheGoMap.

Google Calendar integration

With the owner's permission, Starfleet reads the connected Exercise calendar to show upcoming training and plan workouts. It can add runs and update its marked workouts after an explicit preview and confirmation. The current application does not delete events or invite guests.

This is a privately operated personal-use app, not a public signup service. This informational website does not connect to your calendar or collect running requests.
