STARFLEET

Privacy policy

Effective September 18, 2026. This policy describes the personal running, Spotify and Google Calendar features of Starfleet.

Data accessed and purpose

Starfleet processes Telegram requests, the paired Telegram user/chat identifiers, running preferences, public starting landmarks, route geometry, and workout details to plan routes and respond in the paired chat. Google authorization enables access to events on calendars the authorizing account owns. The application restricts its Calendar operations to the configured Exercise calendar: reading events for training and scheduling conflicts, creating runs, and changing workouts marked as belonging to Starfleet. Changes require an explicit preview confirmation. There is no event-delete command.

Services receiving data

  • Google receives Calendar API requests and manages authorization and calendar storage.
  • Telegram processes bot messages, including route and workout replies.
  • OpenAI receives request text, relevant prior route context, and selected upcoming marked-workout titles, times and description excerpts to interpret requests and propose routes. API requests set store=false; this does not eliminate all provider-side processing or retention under OpenAI's applicable API policies.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim receives place-name queries. The configured Valhalla routing service receives route coordinates for pedestrian routing, elevation and surface checks.
  • Opening an OnTheGoMap route link sends the geometry encoded in that link to OnTheGoMap.

These providers process data under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. Starfleet does not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or use Google user data to train generalized AI models. Google tokens are not included in model prompts or route-service requests.

Spotify playlists

With permission, Starfleet reads playlist metadata and track durations from selected workout playlists and creates new private playlists. It stores its Spotify authorization token and source selections privately on the operator’s computer. Music metadata is not sent to OpenAI; playlist selection uses local duration and role matching. Playlist links and timing summaries are returned through Telegram. The bot does not download audio or control playback. Spotify creation receipts are retained locally for troubleshooting until disconnected or removed by the operator.

Send /music disconnect to remove local Spotify credentials, source selections and creation receipts. You can also revoke access through Spotify account apps. Existing playlists remain in Spotify until you remove them there.

Storage and retention

The operator's computer stores authorization credentials in private files, bot state, pending previews, route context, mapping caches and operational records. Prior route context is used for follow-ups for up to 24 hours; previews expire after 15 minutes. These usage limits are not automatic deletion deadlines. Cached files, saved plan files, operational records and backups may remain until the operator removes them. Calendar events remain in Google Calendar until changed or deleted there. Telegram and other providers apply their own retention policies.

Control and deletion

You may revoke Starfleet's Google access at Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself remove existing calendar events, local data, backups or Telegram history. Contact the operator through the support email shown on the Google consent screen to request deletion of locally retained data. The personal operator may also stop the worker and remove its local credentials and data directly. Calendar events and Telegram messages can be managed in their respective applications.

Limited use and changes

Starfleet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This page will be updated when these features' data practices change.
